How to play
Goal
  • 🔑 Find 5 of 8 keys (glowing beams show nearby ones)
  • 🚪 Keys unlock the exit gate on the north wall. Run through it!
  • 👹 Three monsters hunt you at once by sight and sound. When one spots you, it howls for the others
  • 🗿 In Titan Wasteland, collect power cores to grow GIANT and fight back
Tricks
  • 🌿 Hide in bushes, corn and ball pits
  • 👻 Ghost Potion (Q) makes you invisible · 💥 Flash Bomb (E) stuns monsters
  • ⚡ Speed pads · 🥤 Energy drinks refill stamina
  • 🧱 Jump at a wall, then jump again to wall-jump (rooftops are safe!)
  • 🤸 Parkour: sprint into a low wall to vault it, jump at a ledge to climb up, C to slide (harder to grab), land a big drop while running to roll. Chain moves for combos!
Controls
  • W A S D move · Mouse look (click to lock)
  • Shift sprint · Space jump · C slide · V camera · F flashlight
  • Q ghost · E flash bomb · M map · Esc pause
  • 📱 Touch: left joystick, drag the right side to look
