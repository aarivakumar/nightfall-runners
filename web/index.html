HOW TO PLAY
  • 🔑 Find 5 of 8 keys hidden around the map (glowing beams show nearby ones)
  • 🚪 Keys unlock the exit gate on the north wall, so run through it
  • 👹 Monsters hunt by sight and sound. Sprinting is loud. They can't see far in the woods
  • 🧱 Jump toward a wall in mid-air and press jump again to wall-jump (3 in a row, rooftops are safe!)
  • 🌿 Hide in bushes and corn fields · ⚡ Speed pads · 🥤 Energy drinks refill stamina
  • 👹 All three of a level's monsters hunt you at once. When one spots you, it howls for the others
  • 🧊 Level 2's black ice is slippery · 🌿 Level 3's hedge maze hides you from sight
  • 👥 Multiplayer: one player clicks Create room and shares the code, friends type it and click Join. Keys are shared, the monsters chase whoever is closest
